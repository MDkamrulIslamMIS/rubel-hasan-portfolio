PROFESSIONAL PROFILE

Rubel Hasan | Career Summary

With over 8.7 years of dedicated experience in garments merchandising, product development, and marketing, Rubel Hasan brings extensive expertise in managing high-volume international accounts, technical outerwear execution, and global fashion supply chains.

Currently serving as Senior Merchandiser (Marketing & Merchandising) at JANN Group, he leads the end-to-end merchandising lifecycle for complex outerwear lines including down jackets, seam-sealed ski/snow outerwear, and rainwear.

Prior to JANN Group, Rubel spent four impactful years at Debonair Group as Merchandiser and Assistant Merchandiser, mastering raw material sourcing, time & action scheduling, sample development, quality assurance compliance, and competitive costing negotiations for leading international buyers such as Camel Active, Timberland, Tommy Hilfiger, Columbia, Only & Sons, Nautica, and Kmart.

He holds a B.Sc. in Textile Engineering from Southeast University, majoring in Wet Processing Technology, establishing a deep technical foundation in fabric construction, washing, dyeing, and finishes.
